# Results lineage

## Status labels

- **Reported:** appears in the submitted dissertation or its consolidated
  results record.
- **Recovered code:** executable logic reconstructed from the original working
  files.
- **Replicated:** reserved for results produced by a clean rerun from documented
  inputs. No result currently carries this label.

## Published historical tables

`results/original/model_summary.csv` transcribes the nine-model summary from the
consolidated assessed-results document. The baseline values also appear in the
submitted dissertation: ListenScore about `0.27`, TimeCost about `-0.086`,
Sponsor about `-0.37`, McFadden pseudo-R² about `0.016`, mean log-sum about
`3.48`, and Sponsor-removal delta about `0.27`.

`results/original/welfare_summary.csv` separates the reported log-sum quantities
from their derived ratio. The ratio `mean_delta_cs / mean_cs` is included only
to reproduce the dissertation's descriptive “about 8%” statement. It is not an
identified welfare percentage, monetary value, or causal effect.

`results/original/interaction_summary.csv` records the interaction coefficients
shown in the assessed nine-model summary. Statistical significance is not
reconstructed from coefficient magnitude; standard errors and p-values were not
present in that consolidated table.

## Recovered-runner mismatch

The recovered `notebooks/02_run_all_models.ipynb` computes per-user `CS`,
`CS_noSponsor`, and `delta`, but its final `summary` row does not store their
means. The assessed consolidated results record contains `Mean CS` and
`Mean Delta CS` columns. Therefore the published CSV values are labelled
historical transcriptions, not outputs freshly generated by the recovered
notebook.

This mismatch is preserved and disclosed in v1. It must be resolved during the
clean rerun before the repository is tagged `v1.0-thesis-replication`.

## Source protection

The submitted thesis contains a student identifier and the local podcast export
contains contact/provider fields. Neither source is published. Hashes are kept
locally for audit; the public repository contains only code, documentation, and
aggregate/transcribed results.
