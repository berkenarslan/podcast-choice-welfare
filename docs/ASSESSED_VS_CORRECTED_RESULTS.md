## What changed substantively

The corrected version no longer claims that the data reveal ad aversion,
duration sensitivity, demographic heterogeneity, or real consumer-welfare
effects. Those conclusions cannot be learned from choices generated by the
researcher's own assumed utility function.

The defensible corrected conclusion is:

> Under a declared synthetic data-generating process, the MNL implementation
> recovers the imposed coefficients reasonably well and produces a transparent
> Sponsor=0 log-sum scenario contrast.

For an academic or PhD portfolio, the submitted dissertation should remain a
historical MSc artefact. The corrected branch demonstrates methodological
reflection and reproducible repair, while stronger substantive claims should
come from the separate GenAI research programme or future observed-choice data.
